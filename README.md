# 🌍 EcoGuide AI

### Environmental Education & Natural Farming Assistant

> Learn Today, Protect Tomorrow, Sustain Forever 🌱

---

## 📌 Project Overview

EcoGuide AI is an AI-powered environmental education platform designed to educate people about climate change, pollution, biodiversity conservation, sustainability, and natural farming practices.

The platform acts as a virtual environmental mentor that helps users understand environmental concepts through interactive conversations, educational content, quizzes, and personalized recommendations.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Environmental degradation, climate change, pollution, biodiversity loss, and unsustainable agricultural practices are becoming major global concerns.

Many students, citizens, and farmers:

- Lack awareness about environmental issues.
- Find environmental concepts difficult to understand.
- Do not know how to adopt sustainable practices.
- Have limited access to guidance on natural farming.
- Are unaware of their environmental impact.

There is a need for an intelligent platform that can educate users and provide actionable recommendations for protecting the environment.
